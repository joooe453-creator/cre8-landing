Deposit

You approve exactly the budget to the vault (a separate wallet prompt); unused budget is refunded and a 1% deposit fee applies. Entry needs factory admission, an unpaused open vault, no staged intent and every native position to be priceable.

Request withdrawal

Locks the shares and queues a request. Anyone can partition it; you (or the manager, council or enabled agent) settle each native slice, convert spot claim tokens, then complete for cash or claim in kind. The request id comes from the verified WithdrawalRequested event and is tracked below.
