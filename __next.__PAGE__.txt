<section class="landing-faq" id="faq"><h2>FAQs</h2>
<details open><summary>Where is my money?</summary><p>Assets stay in the vault and its approved portfolio on BNB Chain, as cash, held tokens or supplied positions. Your ERC-4626 shares represent your portion of the vault.</p></details>
<details><summary>What can the agent do?</summary><p>The operator can only act in named markets within the committed limits. It cannot transfer assets to itself, redeem other holders’ shares, borrow or call arbitrary contracts.</p></details>
<details><summary>How do I exit?</summary><p>A cash exit redeems your proportional slice with a minimum received and deadline; failure reverts the transaction. In kind, you receive tokens and lending receipts to manage yourself. External protocols and token transfers can still limit exits.</p></details>
<details><summary>What is an investment limit?</summary><p>A maximum percentage of the vault’s current value for an exact market. Caps total at most 95%. Tightening is immediate; raising waits at least 24 hours. A new market requires a new vault.</p></details>
<details><summary>What happens after a 2% drop?</summary><p>The loss guard compares fee-adjusted share value with the first observation recorded each UTC day and needs risk checks by a keeper. A 2% observed loss blocks new activity. It is not a guaranteed stop-loss or a maximum loss. Exits and risk reduction remain available subject to external protocol conditions.</p></details>
<details><summary>Is this live?</summary><p>The prototype has no verified live vault deployment and cannot accept deposits. Example figures are simulated and the contracts are unaudited. Public supply-rate snapshots have their own sources and timestamps.</p></details>
</section></section>
