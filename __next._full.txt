<ol class="cl-flow-steps">
<li class="cl-flow-step is-on"><p class="cl-flow-count">01 / 05</p><div class="cl-flow-text"><h3>Just ask CRE8 AI</h3>
<p>Tell CRE8 AI what you want in plain words. It finds a fund that fits you, compares your options or drafts a new fund from your idea, then sets it up for you. Nothing moves until you confirm it in your wallet.</p>
<p class="cl-flow-meta"><strong>It can</strong>Find a fund • Compare funds • Draft your own • Review a strategy</p>
<div class="cl-flow-links"><a class="btn" href="https://app.cre8.finance/agent/" data-wipe>Ask CRE8 AI</a></div></div></li>
<li class="cl-flow-step"><p class="cl-flow-count">02 / 05</p><div class="cl-flow-text"><h3>Open a fund</h3>
<p>Pick what your fund can hold, where it can trade and the performance fee you charge. Put in at least 100 USDT of your own to start it. The rules live in the fund contract, and raising a limit or replacing the agent only takes effect after 24 hours' notice.</p>
<p class="cl-flow-meta"><strong>Trades on</strong>PancakeSwap • Venus • Lista • Aave</p>
<div class="cl-flow-links"><a class="btn" href="https://app.cre8.finance/create/" data-wipe>Open a fund</a><a class="cl-flow-more" href="https://app.cre8.finance/agent/" data-wipe>Draft one with CRE8 AI</a></div></div></li>
<li class="cl-flow-step"><p class="cl-flow-count">03 / 05</p><div class="cl-flow-text"><h3>Investors deposit</h3>
<p>Anyone can find your fund on CRE8, read its rules and deposit USDT. The money goes into the fund contract, never to you or the agent, and investors get fund shares in return. The share price is worked out onchain from what the fund holds.</p>
<p class="cl-flow-meta"><strong>Who holds the money</strong>The fund contract, not a person</p>
<div class="cl-flow-links"><a class="btn ghost" href="https://app.cre8.finance/vaults/" data-wipe>Explore funds</a><a class="cl-flow-more" href="https://app.cre8.finance/vault/cre8-usdt/" data-wipe>Read an example fund</a></div></div></li>
<li class="cl-flow-step"><p class="cl-flow-count">04 / 05</p><div class="cl-flow-text"><h3>Your agent runs it</h3>
<p>Your AI agent sends trade orders whenever its strategy calls for one. Before anything executes, the fund contract checks the order against the fund's rules: only the assets and venues you chose, and only within its limits. Orders that break a rule are rejected.</p>
<p class="cl-flow-meta"><strong>The agent can</strong>Swap and lend on the venues you approved</p>
<p class="cl-flow-meta"><strong>The agent can't</strong>Withdraw money or step outside the fund's limits</p></div></li>
<li class="cl-flow-step"><p class="cl-flow-count">05 / 05</p><div class="cl-flow-text"><h3>Everyone gets paid</h3>
<p>Investors can withdraw at the current share price, in cash or in the assets the fund holds. When the fund makes a profit, you earn a performance fee on it. Every trade and fee is onchain, so your track record builds itself.</p>
<p class="cl-flow-meta"><strong>Performance fee</strong>You set it, up to 20% of profit</p>
<div class="cl-flow-links"><a class="cl-textlink" href="#earn">See what you could earn</a></div></div></li>
</ol>
