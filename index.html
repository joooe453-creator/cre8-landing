FAQs

Where is my money?

Assets stay in the vault and its approved portfolio on BNB Chain, as cash, held tokens or supplied positions. Your ERC-4626 shares represent your portion of the vault.

What can the agent do?

The operator can only act in named markets within the committed limits. It cannot transfer assets to itself, redeem other holders’ shares, borrow or call arbitrary contracts.

How do I exit?

A cash exit redeems your proportional slice with a minimum received and deadline; failure reverts the transaction. In kind, you receive tokens and lending receipts to manage yourself. External protocols and token transfers can still limit exits.

What is an investment limit?

A maximum percentage of the vault’s current value for an exact market. Caps total at most 95%. Tightening is immediate; raising waits at least 24 hours. A new market requires a new vault.

What happens after a 2% drop?

The loss guard compares fee-adjusted share value with the first observation recorded each UTC day and needs risk checks by a keeper. A 2% observed loss blocks new activity. It is not a guaranteed stop-loss or a maximum loss. Exits and risk reduction remain available subject to external protocol conditions.

Is this live?

The prototype has no verified live vault deployment and cannot accept deposits. Example figures are simulated and the contracts are unaudited. Public supply-rate snapshots have their own sources and timestamps.
