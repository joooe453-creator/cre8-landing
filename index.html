  1. 01 / 04

    Open a fund

    Write a thesis, pick what it can hold, where it trades and your fee, then seed it with at least 100 USDT.

    Write the thesis in one sentence, then pick what your fund can hold, where it can trade and the performance fee you charge. Put in at least 100 USDT of your own to start it. The mandate lives in the fund contract, and raising a limit or replacing the agent only takes effect after 24 hours' notice.

    Trades onPancakeSwap • Venus • Lista • Aave

    Open a fundOr just ask CRE8 AI

  2. 02 / 04

    Holders deposit

    Anyone can deposit USDT. The money sits in the fund contract, never with you or the agent.

    Anyone can find your fund on CRE8, read its thesis and mandate, and deposit USDT. The money goes into the fund contract, never to you or the agent, and holders get fund shares in return. The share price is worked out onchain from what the fund holds.

    Who holds the moneyThe fund contract, not a person

    Explore fundsRead an example fund

  3. 03 / 04

    It runs by its mandate

    A plan or your AI agent places the orders, and the fund contract checks each one against the mandate first.

    The fund follows its plan, such as weekly buys or a quarterly rebalance, or your AI agent decides when to trade. Before anything executes, the fund contract checks the order against the mandate: only the assets and venues you chose, and only within its limits. Orders that break a rule are rejected.

    The plan or agent canSwap and lend on the venues you approved

    Nobody canWithdraw holders’ money or step outside the mandate

  4. 04 / 04

    Everyone gets paid

    Holders withdraw at the share price. You earn a performance fee when the fund makes a profit.

    Holders can withdraw at the current share price, as USDT or as the assets the fund holds. When the fund makes a profit, you earn a performance fee on it. Every trade and fee is onchain, so your track record builds itself.

    Performance feeYou set it, up to 20% of profit
