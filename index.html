  1. 01 / 05

    Just ask CRE8 AI

    Tell CRE8 AI what you want in plain words. It finds a fund that fits you, compares your options or drafts a new fund from your idea, then sets it up for you. Nothing moves until you confirm it in your wallet.

    It canFind a fund • Compare funds • Draft your own • Review a strategy

    Ask CRE8 AI

  2. 02 / 05

    Open a fund

    Pick what your fund can hold, where it can trade and the performance fee you charge. Put in at least 100 USDT of your own to start it. The rules live in the fund contract, and raising a limit or replacing the agent only takes effect after 24 hours' notice.

    Trades onPancakeSwap • Venus • Lista • Aave

    Open a fundDraft one with CRE8 AI

  3. 03 / 05

    Investors deposit

    Anyone can find your fund on CRE8, read its rules and deposit USDT. The money goes into the fund contract, never to you or the agent, and investors get fund shares in return. The share price is worked out onchain from what the fund holds.

    Who holds the moneyThe fund contract, not a person

    Explore fundsRead an example fund

  4. 04 / 05

    Your agent runs it

    Your AI agent sends trade orders whenever its strategy calls for one. Before anything executes, the fund contract checks the order against the fund's rules: only the assets and venues you chose, and only within its limits. Orders that break a rule are rejected.

    The agent canSwap and lend on the venues you approved

    The agent can'tWithdraw money or step outside the fund's limits

  5. 05 / 05

    Everyone gets paid

    Investors can withdraw at the current share price, in cash or in the assets the fund holds. When the fund makes a profit, you earn a performance fee on it. Every trade and fee is onchain, so your track record builds itself.

    Performance feeYou set it, up to 20% of profit
